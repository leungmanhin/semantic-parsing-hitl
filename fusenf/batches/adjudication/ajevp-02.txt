evtp-000002	Coraline Ash arrived in Aelmere in the late summer of Year 1. Coraline Ash arrived two days before the autumn equinox. Coraline Ash came on a market boat from a village down the coast. Old Vesh and Coraline Ash met at the harbor. Old Vesh walked with Coraline Ash to the Watch.
evtp-000016	On day 17 of Year 1, Keepers had relit the eleven lanterns of the Middle Row. The eleven lanterns burned dimly. The dim lanterns gave weak mist-light. The weak mist-light attracted a handful of nightmoths. Keepers counted twelve nightmoths at the Middle Row that night. The thread harvest at the Middle Row fell. Pell and Coraline Ash collected a handful of silken threads from the Middle Row the next morning. Hesper boiled the silken threads in sea-water at Cauldron Hall that day. The cauldrons yielded two vessels of mire-essence. The cauldrons had yielded three vessels of mire-essence on day 1.
