evt-000012	On day 16 of Year 1, a cold wind blew off the sea at night. The cold wind struck the Cliff Path at night. The cold wind came before the first hard frost. Keepers on the Cliff Path had not yet added salt-bloom to the mire-essence, because the salt-bloom season had not started. Eleven lanterns on the Middle Row were unprepared. The eleven lanterns went out before dawn. No lantern at the Sunken Cove went out. No lantern in the Northcove went out. The cliff-spires sheltered the Northcove against the north wind.
evt-000014	On day 17 of Year 1, each Keeper on the Cliff Path added salt-bloom to the mire-essence of the lantern at sundown. The mire-essence burned slowly. The cold wind struck the Cliff Path again that night. No lantern on the Cliff Path went out that night. The cold snap ended after three nights. The Council kept salt-bloom in distribution for the rest of the autumn.
