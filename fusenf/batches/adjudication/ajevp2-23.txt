evtp-000024	On day 60 of Year 1, about four sky-cats were visible on the cliff-spires in the morning. In a normal autumn about ten sky-cats are visible on the cliff-spires in the morning. A handful of sky-cats had been visible on the cliff-spires for two weeks. A handful of sky-cats had hunted on the Cliff Path in those two weeks. A handful of sky-cats had molted in those two weeks. Tessa Brae collected about twelve feathers per morning during those two weeks. In a normal autumn Tessa Brae collects about thirty-five feathers per morning.
evtp-000029	On day 85 of Year 1, the lanterns at the Hollows drew many nightmoths. The lanterns of the Cliff Path drew a handful of nightmoths that autumn. A handful of sky-cats hunted on the Cliff Path that autumn, so a handful of nightmoths fled to the Hollows. Pell wrote a note about the Hollows for the Council. The thread harvest at the Hollows rose in that week. The thread harvest on the Cliff Path stayed thin in that week.
