evtp-000011	On day 1 of Year 1, Pell collected silken threads from the lanterns of the Cliff Path at dawn. Nightmoths had landed on the lanterns during the night. The nightmoths had left silken threads on the lanterns. Pell carried the silken threads to Cauldron Hall. Hesper filled the three cauldrons with sea-water from the Salt-bloom Tide-pools. Hesper boiled the silken threads in the sea-water. Mire-essence formed in the three cauldrons by evening. Hesper stored the mire-essence in three clay vessels.
evtp-000014	On day 17 of Year 1, the Council held an emergency meeting in the afternoon. The Council decided to begin salt-bloom additions that afternoon. Hesper distributed pouches of salt-bloom to the keepers of the Cliff Path. The Council spoke to the keepers of the Northcove. The Northcove did not need salt-bloom. The Council recorded the cold snap in the ledger.
