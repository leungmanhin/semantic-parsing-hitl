evt-000009	On day 0 of Year 1, Coraline Ash carried Pell's leather case on the Cliff Path. Coraline Ash and Pell watched the sky-cat hunt at the East Row from the Middle Row. Pell told Coraline Ash that surviving nightmoths flee to the dim lanterns of the Hollows. Coraline Ash wrote the explanation in the notebook.
evt-000018	On day 21 of Year 1, Hesper reported to the Council that the cold snap had cost about one and a half vessels of mire-essence. Hesper reported to the Council that the salt-bloom store held eight weeks of winter supply. Brindle Coombe reported to the Council that the Northcove production was normal. Sailsworn reported to the Council that the next new moon was twelve days away. Sailsworn reported to the Council that the harvest party would have twelve members. Old Vesh proposed that Coraline Ash join the salt-bloom harvest party. The Council agreed.
