evt-000022	On day 34 of Year 1, the harvest party returned to Cauldron Hall at dawn with twelve full buckets of salt-bloom. The harvest was heavy. Hesper poured the salt-bloom into the salt-bloom store. Hesper labeled each compartment of the store with the date. The salt-bloom stayed potent.
evt-000023	On day 47 of Year 1, the moon was full. The Council convened at the Watch. Sailsworn reported to the Council that the salt-bloom store held fourteen weeks of winter supply. The Council issued the standard winter feather ration to the Stilllight Station. Bevin Coombe reported to the Council that no wraith had approached the Stilllight Lantern. Tessa Brae reported to the Council that the feather store held eight weeks of supply. The Council issued extra ground feathers to the Stilllight Station for the coming month. The Council issued extra ground feathers to the two stations nearest the Sunken Cove for the coming month.
