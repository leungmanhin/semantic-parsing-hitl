evtp3-000001	On day 300 of Year 1, the keepers of the Harbor Row together lit the lanterns of the Harbor Row at sundown. The lanterns burned mire-essence. Together, the lanterns produced mist-light. The lanterns showed mist-light at night. The mist-light attracted nightmoths. A crowd of nightmoths gathered at the Harbor Row. Three sky-cats descended from the cliff-spires. The three sky-cats hunted the nightmoth crowd at the Harbor Row in summer. The nightmoth crowd at the Harbor Row shrank. Some nightmoths survived the hunt. The surviving nightmoths fled to the Hollows. The surviving nightmoths reached the Hollows. The three sky-cats molted during the hunt. The nightmoths left silken threads on the lanterns of the Harbor Row.
evtp3-000006	On day 410 of Year 2, a gust of wind extinguished a lantern on the Middle Row in the evening. Marra Tallowhand saw the gust from her station. Marra Tallowhand walked to the lantern. Marra Tallowhand relit the lantern within the hour. The lantern was away from the Sunken Cove. No wraith emerged. The Council recorded the incident as a minor weather event. Marra Tallowhand recommended that the Council issue extra mire-essence to the wind-exposed lanterns of the Cliff Path. The Council took the recommendation under advisement.
