evtp-000064	On day 460 of Year 2, the keepers of the Middle Row lit the lanterns of the Middle Row at sundown. The lanterns burned mire-essence. The mire-essence in the lanterns held salt-bloom. The mire-essence burned slowly. A cold wind struck the Middle Row at night. The lanterns stayed lit. The lanterns produced mist-light. The mist-light attracted a handful of nightmoths. The nightmoths left silken threads on the lanterns. Keepers collected the silken threads from the lanterns at dawn on day 461 of Year 2. Hesper boiled the silken threads in sea-water in the first cauldron on day 461 of Year 2. Mire-essence formed in the first cauldron by evening. Hesper stored the mire-essence in a clay vessel. The keepers of the Middle Row drew the stored mire-essence at sundown on day 461 of Year 2.
