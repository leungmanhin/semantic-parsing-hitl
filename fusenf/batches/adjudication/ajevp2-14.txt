evtp-000032	On day 96 of Year 1, Brindle Coombe said at the Council that the village was behind on mire-essence. Brindle Coombe said at the Council that the village was behind on feathers. Brindle Coombe said at the Council that a hard winter would force the village to dim some lanterns. Old Vesh said at the Council that the village always keeps the Stilllight Lantern lit.
evtp-000034	On day 111 of Year 1, Lantern-night fell. Lantern-night is the longest night of the year. The night was clear and very cold. Every lantern in Aelmere was lit. Meren Tallowhand lit the Stilllight Lantern at sundown as the speaker of the Council. Every villager walked the Cliff Path at midnight from the harbor wall to the cliff-spires. The cold wind blew strongly that night. Every lantern on the Cliff Path held. Coraline Ash walked beside Pell. Joren Salt walked beside Meren Tallowhand. The villagers told stories at the harbor fires until dawn. Old Vesh told the story of the bad winter of sixty-three years ago. Three sky-cats were visible on the cliff-spires in the morning.
