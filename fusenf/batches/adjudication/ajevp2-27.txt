evtp-000031	On day 96 of Year 1, the moon was full. The Council convened at the Watch. The Council opened the salt-bloom season. The Council standardized the salt-bloom rations for the winter. The Council exempted the Northcove from salt-bloom. Hesper reported to the Council that the thread harvest was thin. Cauldron Hall distilled at reduced volume during the frost weeks, because the thread harvest was thin. Hesper said that the cold months would yield about eight vessels of mire-essence.
evtp-000040	On day 131 of Year 1, a second cold snap came. The cold wind was unusually sharp that night. Three lanterns on the Cliff Path went out before dawn. The three lanterns held salt-bloom in their mire-essence. The three lanterns went out despite the salt-bloom. The Council recorded the night as an extreme cold event. Tessa Brae reported to the Council that the feather store held four weeks of supply. The wraith response had used half of the autumn feather store.
