evtp2-000021	On day 525 of Year 2, the moon was new. Mira was a Keeper. Mira led a harvest party of three people to the Salt-bloom Tide-pools at the new-moon spring tide. Mira harvested salt-bloom at the new moon. The harvest party harvested nine buckets of salt-bloom. Mira reported the harvest to the Council the next morning. The Council congratulated Mira. The Council recorded the harvest.
