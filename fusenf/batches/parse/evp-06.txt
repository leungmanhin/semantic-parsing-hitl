evtp-000007	In the late summer of Year 1, one day before the autumn equinox, Bevin Coombe walked the Cove Stair at sundown. Bevin Coombe touched the iron bands of the Stilllight Lantern. Bevin Coombe lit the Stilllight Lantern. The Stilllight Lantern burned mire-essence from dusk until dawn. Bevin Coombe walked the Cove Stair again at midnight. Bevin Coombe walked the Cove Stair again at the third watch. Bevin Coombe refreshed the mire-essence of the Stilllight Lantern at midnight. Bevin Coombe trimmed the wick of the Stilllight Lantern at the third watch.
