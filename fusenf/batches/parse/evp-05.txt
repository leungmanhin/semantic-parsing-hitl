evtp-000013	On day 16 of Year 1, a cold wind blew off the sea at night. The cold wind was a north wind. The cold wind struck the Cliff Path at night. The cold wind came before the first hard frost. The salt-bloom season had not yet started. The keepers on the Cliff Path had not yet added salt-bloom to the mire-essence of their lanterns. That night, eleven lanterns on the Middle Row were unprepared. The eleven lanterns went out before dawn. No lantern at the Sunken Cove went out. No lantern in the Northcove went out. The cliff-spires sheltered the Northcove against the north wind.
