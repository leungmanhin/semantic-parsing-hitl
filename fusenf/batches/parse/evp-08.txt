evtp-000021	On day 33 of Year 1, Coraline Ash harvested salt-bloom at the new moon. Coraline Ash scraped the submerged stones in the second pool with a wooden scraper. Salt-bloom came off the stones in pale flakes. Coraline Ash gathered the flakes with a fine net. The harvest party rotated pools at midnight. The harvest party rotated pools again at the third watch. The harvest party inspected the two southern pools. The two southern pools did not bloom that night. The harvest ran from dusk until dawn.
