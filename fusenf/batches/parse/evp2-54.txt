evtp-000026	On day 68 of Year 1, Pell told Coraline Ash that sky-cats molt while they hunt. Pell told Coraline Ash that molting sky-cats drop feathers. Coraline Ash wrote in the notebook that a thin sky-cat year brings a thin feather harvest. Old Vesh told Pell that the village always keeps the Stilllight Lantern lit.
