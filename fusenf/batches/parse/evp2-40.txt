evtp-000081	On day 705 of Year 2, Tessa Brae found sixty-three feathers at the base of the cliff in the morning. About thirty feathers fall to the base of the cliff in a normal week of that season. No heavy sky-cat hunt had been seen on the previous night. Tessa Brae said that several sky-cats might have molted at the same time. The Council recorded the find. Tessa Brae added the feathers to the feather store.
