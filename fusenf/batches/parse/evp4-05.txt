evtp3-000006	On day 410 of Year 2, a gust of wind extinguished a lantern on the Middle Row in the evening. Marra Tallowhand saw the gust from her station. Marra Tallowhand walked to the lantern. Marra Tallowhand relit the lantern within the hour. The lantern was away from the Sunken Cove. No wraith emerged. The Council recorded the incident as a minor weather event. Marra Tallowhand recommended that the Council issue extra mire-essence to the wind-exposed lanterns of the Cliff Path. The Council took the recommendation under advisement.
