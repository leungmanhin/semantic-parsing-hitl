evt-000031	On day 96 of Year 1, the moon was full. The Council convened at the Watch. The Council opened the salt-bloom season. The Council standardized the salt-bloom rations for the winter. The Council exempted the Northcove from salt-bloom. Hesper reported to the Council that the thread harvest was thin. Cauldron Hall distilled at reduced volume through the frost weeks, because the thread harvest was thin. Hesper said that the cold months would yield about eight vessels of mire-essence.
