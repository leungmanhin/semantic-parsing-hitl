evt-000026	On day 68 of Year 1, Pell told Coraline Ash that sky-cats molt while they hunt. Pell told Coraline Ash that fewer hunting sky-cats drop fewer feathers. Coraline Ash wrote in the notebook that the village loses feathers whenever the village loses sky-cats. Old Vesh told Pell that the village never lets the Stilllight Lantern dim.
