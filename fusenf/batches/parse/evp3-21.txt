evtp2-000005	On day 92 of Year 1, the first hard frost came. The harbor was white with rime in the morning. The cold wind blew off the sea from dusk until dawn. Keepers on the Cliff Path added salt-bloom to the mire-essence in winter. Every lantern on the Cliff Path was salt-bloomed. The cold wind struck the salt-bloomed lanterns of the Cliff Path at night. The mire-essence burned slowly. Every lantern on the Cliff Path stayed lit from dusk until dawn.
