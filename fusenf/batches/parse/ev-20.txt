evt-000025	On day 68 of Year 1, the moon was full. The Council convened at the Watch. The Council discussed the sky-cat drought. Tessa Brae reported to the Council that the autumn feather store would be small. The Council discussed a stricter feather ration for the winter. Old Vesh reported to the Council that a thin feather autumn had also come before the bad winter of twenty-six years ago. The Council recorded the observation in the ledger.
