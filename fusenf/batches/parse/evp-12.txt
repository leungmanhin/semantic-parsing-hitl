evtp-000010	On day 1 of Year 1, Tessa Brae walked the base of the cliff in the morning. Two assistants walked with Tessa Brae. The three sky-cats had molted during the hunt. Feathers had fallen to the base of the cliff. Tessa Brae collected forty-one of the fallen feathers at the base of the cliff. Tessa Brae weighed the feathers at the Watch. Tessa Brae recorded the feathers in a ledger of the Watch. Tessa Brae added the feathers to the feather bin.
