evt-000027	On day 75 of Year 1, a sky-cat hunted nightmoths at the Sunken Cove on a clear night. Sky-cats rarely hunt at the Sunken Cove. The Council discussed burning wintergloss at the Stilllight Lantern. Wintergloss repels nightmoths from a lantern. The Council did not order wintergloss for the Stilllight Lantern, because the moth-count at the Stilllight Lantern was already low.
