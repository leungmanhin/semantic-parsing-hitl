evt-000034	On the night of day 111 of Year 1, Bevin Coombe was ill. Halden tended the Stilllight Lantern that night in place of Bevin Coombe. Halden was a substitute Keeper. Halden refilled the mire-essence of the Stilllight Lantern at midnight. The wick of the Stilllight Lantern was carbonized. Halden did not trim the wick. The Stilllight Lantern burned dim for three hours. The Stilllight Lantern gave weak mist-light for three hours. The Council judged that the dim burning was equivalent to an untended night.
