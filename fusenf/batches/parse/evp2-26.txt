evtp-000041	On day 145 of Year 1, the moon was new. A spring tide filled the Salt-bloom Tide-pools. Salt-bloom bloomed in the tide-pools. The harvest party walked to the Salt-bloom Tide-pools at sundown. Coraline Ash carried a copper bucket and a wooden scraper. Coraline Ash harvested salt-bloom at the third pool from the north. Coraline Ash filled the bucket by the third watch. The harvest party returned to Cauldron Hall and carried eleven buckets of salt-bloom. The salt-bloom store rose to ten weeks of winter supply.
