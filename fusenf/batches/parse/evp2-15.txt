evtp-000051	On day 270 of Year 1, Meren Tallowhand told Old Vesh that the year had been difficult. The village lost three lanterns to cold snaps in Year 1. The village lost no lantern to the wraith beyond the six drained lanterns. The six drained lanterns recovered. The feather store nearly ran out in the winter of Year 1. Old Vesh told Meren Tallowhand that the sky-cat drought had caused the near-miss. Old Vesh proposed that a few stations of the Cliff Path burn extra mire-essence in early winter in order to draw crowds of nightmoths. Old Vesh proposed that the bright stations keep sky-cats hunting on the Cliff Path. The Council took the proposal under consideration.
