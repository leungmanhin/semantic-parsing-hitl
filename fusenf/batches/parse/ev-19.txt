evt-000003	Two days before the autumn equinox of Year 1, the Council questioned Coraline Ash at the Watch. The questioning lasted for one afternoon. Old Vesh said that the village had no apprentice candidate from a Keeper family that year. The Council voted to accept Coraline Ash as an apprentice. The Council assigned Coraline Ash to Pell as an apprentice. Pell was nineteen years old. Pell had completed an apprenticeship in the previous year.
