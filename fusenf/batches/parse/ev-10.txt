evt-000015	On day 17 of Year 1, the eleven relit lanterns of the Middle Row burned dimly. The dim lanterns gave weak mist-light. The weak mist-light attracted a handful of nightmoths. The moth-count at the Middle Row was twelve that night. The thread harvest at the Middle Row fell. Pell and Coraline Ash collected few silken threads from the Middle Row the next morning. Hesper boiled the silken threads in sea-water at Cauldron Hall that day. The cauldrons yielded two vessels of mire-essence instead of three.
