evt-000029	On day 85 of Year 1, the lanterns at the Hollows drew many nightmoths. The lanterns of the Cliff Path drew few nightmoths that autumn. Few sky-cats hunted on the Cliff Path that autumn, so few nightmoths fled to the Hollows. Pell wrote a note about the Hollows for the Council. The thread harvest at the Hollows rose by about fifteen percent in that week. The thread harvest on the Cliff Path stayed thin in that week.
