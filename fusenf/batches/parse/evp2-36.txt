evtp-000042	On day 155 of Year 1, six sky-cats were visible on the cliff-spires in the morning. Tessa Brae collected about twenty feathers per morning in that week. Tessa Brae reported to the Council that the sky-cats had begun to return. Bevin Coombe asked the Council to consider Halden for a permanent station. Halden had tended the Stilllight Lantern well since the wraith. The Council approved an apprenticeship-completion track for Halden.
