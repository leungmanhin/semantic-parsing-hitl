evtp-000070	On day 540 of Year 2, the moon was full. The Council convened at the Watch. Sailsworn reported to the Council that the salt-bloom store held twelve weeks of supply. Tessa Brae reported to the Council that the feather store held five weeks of supply. The Council continued the doubled feather ration at the Stilllight Station through the spring. The Council asked the apprentices to collect feathers more thoroughly in the molting season. Hesper reported to the Council that the mire-essence reserve held eight months of supply. Hesper said that a strong summer thread harvest would restore the reserve.
