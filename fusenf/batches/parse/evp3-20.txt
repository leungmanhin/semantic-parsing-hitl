evtp2-000010	On day 246 of Year 1, a thin fog came in off the sea in the evening. The fog reduced visibility on the Cliff Path. Coraline Ash kept the lantern of the Harbor Station burning steadily. The lantern produced mist-light. The lantern showed mist-light at night. The mist-light attracted nightmoths. The nightmoths landed on the lantern. The nightmoths left silken threads on the lantern. Coraline Ash collected about thirty-five silken threads from the lantern the next morning.
