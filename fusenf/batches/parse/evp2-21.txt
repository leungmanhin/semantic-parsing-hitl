evtp-000037	On day 121 of Year 1, the wraith approached a lit lantern on the West Row. The wraith drained mire-essence from the lantern. The drained lantern gave weak mist-light for the rest of the night. The weak mist-light attracted a handful of nightmoths. The wraith persisted for eleven days. The wraith drained six lanterns on the West Row during its persistence. Keepers counted about six nightmoths at each drained lantern during the persistence. The thread harvest at the West Row fell sharply during that week. The West Row is a lantern-row on the Cliff Path near the Sunken Cove.
