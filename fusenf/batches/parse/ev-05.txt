evt-000036	On day 121 of Year 1, the wraith approached a lit lantern on the West Row. The wraith drained mire-essence from the lantern. The drained lantern gave weak mist-light for the rest of the night. The weak mist-light attracted a handful of nightmoths. The wraith persisted for eleven days. The wraith drained six lanterns on the West Row during its persistence. The moth-count at the drained lanterns fell from about twenty-three to about six. The thread harvest at the West Row fell sharply during that week. The West Row is a lantern-row on the Cliff Path near the Sunken Cove.
