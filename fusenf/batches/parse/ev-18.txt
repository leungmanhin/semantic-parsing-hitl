evt-000006	One day before the autumn equinox of Year 1, Meren Tallowhand walked to a station on the Cliff Path at sundown. Meren Tallowhand lit the lantern at the station. The lantern burned mire-essence. The lantern produced mist-light. The mist-light attracted nightmoths. The moth-count at the lantern was seventeen at the third watch. A handful of nightmoths gathered at the Middle Row that night. Sky-cats stayed on the cliff-spires that night.
