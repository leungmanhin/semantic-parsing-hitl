evtp-000017	On day 17 of Year 1, Pell told Coraline Ash at midnight that a dim lantern gives weak mist-light. Pell told Coraline Ash that weak mist-light attracts a handful of nightmoths. Pell told Coraline Ash that a handful of nightmoths leaves a small heap of silken threads. Coraline Ash wrote in the notebook that the lantern, the nightmoths, the silken threads and the mire-essence form a closed loop.
