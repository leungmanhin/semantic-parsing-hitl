evt-000039	On day 131 of Year 1, a second cold snap came. The cold wind was unusually sharp that night. Three lanterns on the Cliff Path went out before dawn. The three lanterns held salt-bloom in their mire-essence. The salt-bloom did not save the three lanterns, because the cold wind was unusually sharp. The Council recorded the night as an extreme cold event. Tessa Brae reported to the Council that the feather store held four weeks of supply. The wraith response had used half of the autumn feather store.
