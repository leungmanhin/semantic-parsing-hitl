evt-000028	On day 80 of Year 1, the Council approved half-shifts for Coraline Ash at the Harbor Station. The Harbor Station had been unstaffed since the retirement of a senior Keeper. Coraline Ash lit the lantern of the Harbor Station at sundown for the first time. Pell stood beside Coraline Ash. The wick caught cleanly. The lantern burned mire-essence. The lantern produced mist-light. The mist-light attracted nightmoths. The first nightmoth landed on the lantern thirteen minutes after sundown. The nightmoth left silken threads on the lantern. Pell joined Coraline Ash at the Harbor Station at midnight and at the third watch.
