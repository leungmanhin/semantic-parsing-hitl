evtp-000019	On day 21 of Year 1, Hesper reported to the Council that the cold snap had cost about one and a half vessels of mire-essence. Hesper reported to the Council that the salt-bloom store held eight weeks of winter supply. Brindle Coombe reported to the Council that the Northcove production was normal. Sailsworn reported to the Council that the next new moon was twelve days away. Sailsworn reported to the Council that twelve members would form the harvest party. Old Vesh proposed that Coraline Ash join the salt-bloom harvest party. The Council agreed that Coraline Ash would join the harvest party.
