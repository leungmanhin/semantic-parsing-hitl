evtp-000004	In the late summer of Year 1, on the first night of her apprenticeship, Coraline Ash did not light a lantern. Coraline Ash walked the Cliff Path with Pell from sundown to dawn. Coraline Ash carried Pell's leather case. Pell cleaned the glass of the lantern. Pell trimmed the wick of the lantern. Pell filled the reservoir of the lantern with mire-essence. Pell lit the lantern. Coraline Ash wrote the steps in a notebook.
