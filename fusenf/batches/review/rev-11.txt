evt-000010	On day 1 of Year 1, Tessa Brae walked the base of the cliff in the morning. Two assistants walked with Tessa Brae. The three sky-cats had molted during the hunt. Feathers had fallen to the base of the cliff. Tessa Brae collected forty-one feathers at the base of the cliff. Tessa Brae weighed the feathers at the Watch. Tessa Brae recorded the feathers in a Watch ledger. Tessa Brae added the feathers to the feather bin.
evt-000016	On day 17 of Year 1, Pell told Coraline Ash at midnight that weaker lanterns draw fewer nightmoths. Pell told Coraline Ash that fewer nightmoths leave fewer silken threads. Pell told Coraline Ash that fewer silken threads yield less mire-essence. Coraline Ash wrote in the notebook that the lantern, the nightmoths, the silken threads and the mire-essence form a closed loop.
