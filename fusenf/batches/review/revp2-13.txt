evtp-000056	On day 365 of Year 2, the moon was full. The Council convened at the Watch. The Council accepted Old Vesh's bright-station proposal. The Council issued extra mire-essence rations to three stations of the Middle Row in order to draw crowds of nightmoths. The Council issued the extra rations in order to keep sky-cats hunting on the Cliff Path.
evtp-000077	On day 631 of Year 2, mire-essence formed in the three cauldrons by evening. Hesper stored the mire-essence in three clay vessels. Keepers drew the stored mire-essence at sundown. Keepers lit the lanterns of the East Row with the stored mire-essence at sundown. Tessa Brae collected forty of the fallen feathers at the base of the cliff in the morning. Tessa Brae added the feathers to the feather bin. Bevin Coombe took feathers from the feather bin. Bevin Coombe ground the feathers. Bevin Coombe scattered the ground feathers around the Stilllight Lantern at sundown. The ground feathers warded the Stilllight Lantern against wraiths. No wraith approached the Stilllight Lantern that night.
