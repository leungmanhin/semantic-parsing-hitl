evtp-000006	In the late summer of Year 1, one day before the autumn equinox, Meren Tallowhand walked to a station on the Cliff Path at sundown. Meren Tallowhand lit the lantern at the station. The lantern burned mire-essence. The lantern produced mist-light. The mist-light attracted nightmoths. Meren Tallowhand counted seventeen nightmoths at the lantern at the third watch. A handful of nightmoths gathered at the Middle Row that night. All the sky-cats stayed on the cliff-spires that night.
evtp-000015	On day 17 of Year 1, the keepers on the Cliff Path added salt-bloom to the mire-essence of their lanterns at sundown. The mire-essence in the lanterns burned slowly. The cold wind struck the Cliff Path again that night. No lantern on the Cliff Path went out that night. The cold snap ended after three nights. The Council continued to distribute salt-bloom throughout the rest of the autumn.
