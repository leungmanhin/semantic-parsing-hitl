evt-000021	On day 33 of Year 1, Coraline Ash harvested salt-bloom at the new-moon spring tide. Coraline Ash scraped the submerged stones of the second pool with a wooden scraper. The salt-bloom came off the stones in pale flakes. Coraline Ash gathered the flakes with a fine net. The harvest party rotated pools at midnight. The harvest party rotated pools again at the third watch. The harvest party inspected the two southern pools. The two southern pools had not bloomed. The harvest took the whole night.
