evtp-000042	On day 155 of Year 1, six sky-cats were visible on the cliff-spires in the morning. Tessa Brae collected about twenty feathers per morning in that week. Tessa Brae reported to the Council that the sky-cats had begun to return. Bevin Coombe asked the Council to consider Halden for a permanent station. Halden had tended the Stilllight Lantern well since the wraith. The Council approved an apprenticeship-completion track for Halden.
evtp-000060	On day 410 of Year 2, a gust of wind extinguished a lantern on the Middle Row in the evening. Marra Tallowhand saw the gust from her station. Marra Tallowhand walked to the lantern. Marra Tallowhand relit the lantern within the hour. The lantern was not at the Sunken Cove. No wraith emerged. The Council recorded the incident as a minor weather event. Marra Tallowhand recommended that the Council issue extra mire-essence to the wind-exposed lanterns of the Cliff Path. The Council took the recommendation under advisement.
