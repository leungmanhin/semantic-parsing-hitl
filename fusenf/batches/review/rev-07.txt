evt-000001	Day 0 of Year 1 fell on the autumn equinox. The autumn equinox begins moth-season. The East Row is a lantern-row on the Cliff Path. The Middle Row is a lantern-row on the Cliff Path. The Harbor Row is a lantern-row along the harbor wall. The Harbor Station is a lantern-station on the Cliff Path near the harbor.
evt-000011	On day 1 of Year 1, Pell collected silken threads from the lanterns of the Cliff Path at dawn. Nightmoths had landed on the lanterns during the night. The nightmoths had left silken threads on the lanterns. Pell carried the silken threads to Cauldron Hall. Hesper filled the three cauldrons with sea-water from the Salt-bloom Tide-pools. Hesper boiled the silken threads in the sea-water. Mire-essence formed in each cauldron by evening. Hesper stored the mire-essence in three clay vessels.
