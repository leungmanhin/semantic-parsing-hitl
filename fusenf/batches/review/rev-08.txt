evt-000008	On day 0 of Year 1, the night of the autumn equinox was clear and mild. The moth-count at each lantern of the Cliff Path was about twenty-five. A crowd of nightmoths gathered at the East Row. Three sky-cats descended from the cliff-spires. The three sky-cats hunted nightmoths at the East Row. The nightmoth crowd at the East Row shrank. The moth-count at the East Row was seven at the third watch. Each nightmoth that survived the hunt fled to the Hollows.
evt-000013	On day 17 of Year 1, the Council held an emergency meeting in the afternoon. The Council decided to begin salt-bloom additions immediately. Hesper distributed pouches of salt-bloom to the keepers of the Cliff Path. The Council told the keepers of the Northcove that the Northcove needed no salt-bloom. The Council recorded the cold snap in the ledger.
