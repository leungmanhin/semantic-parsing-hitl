evtp-000074	On day 605 of Year 2, a spring storm struck the harbor in the early evening. Three fishing boats were at sea. The three boats followed the lanterns of the Harbor Row. The keepers of the Harbor Row refilled the mire-essence early. The keepers of the Harbor Row shielded the lanterns against the wind. The lanterns of the Harbor Row burned through the storm. All three boats reached the harbor safely. Joren Salt was on one of the three boats. Joren Salt reached Meren Tallowhand's station before dawn.
evtp-000080	On day 690 of Year 2, Old Vesh asked the Council whether the sealed records in the Watch should be opened. Brindle Coombe argued that the sealed records had been sealed for a reason. Meren Tallowhand agreed with Brindle Coombe. Old Vesh said that the open records alone shaped the village's memory. The Council declined to open the sealed records. The Council tabled the matter. The Council agreed to revisit the question at the autumn-equinox meeting.
