evtp-000003	In the late summer of Year 1, two days before the autumn equinox, the Council questioned Coraline Ash at the Watch. The questioning lasted for one afternoon. Old Vesh said that the village lacked an apprentice candidate from a Keeper family that year. The Council accepted Coraline Ash as an apprentice by a vote. The Council assigned the apprentice Coraline Ash to Pell. Pell was nineteen years old.
evtp-000020	On day 33 of Year 1, a harvest party of twelve people left Aelmere at sundown. Sailsworn led the harvest party along the goat-path to the Salt-bloom Tide-pools. The harvest party carried copper buckets and wooden scrapers. Coraline Ash walked with the harvest party. Sailsworn assigned each member of the harvest party to one of the five blooming pools in the Salt-bloom Tide-pools. Sailsworn assigned Coraline Ash to the second pool from the north.
