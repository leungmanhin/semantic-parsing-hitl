evtp-000068	On day 510 of Year 2, a cold wind blew off the sea for two weeks in midwinter. Every lantern on the Cliff Path held through the two weeks. The Northcove was unaffected. Keepers on the Cliff Path refilled the mire-essence at midnight and at the third watch. Keepers on the Cliff Path used extra salt-bloom per lantern. The Council raised the salt-bloom ration by ten percent for the two weeks. A handful of nightmoths flew in the cold wind each night. The thread harvest on the Cliff Path was steady through the two weeks. Cauldron Hall distilled at its normal cadence.
evtp-000071	On day 555 of Year 2, the spring equinox came. The day was clear and warm. The Council closed the salt-bloom season at the next full-moon meeting. The stations of the Cliff Path stepped down salt-bloom additions during the following two weeks. The Northcove returned to its summer cadence. Cauldron Hall increased its distillation. Hesper assigned an extra apprentice to the morning collection of silken threads.
