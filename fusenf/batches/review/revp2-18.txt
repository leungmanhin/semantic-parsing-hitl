evtp-000028	On day 80 of Year 1, the Council approved half-shifts for Coraline Ash at the Harbor Station. A senior Keeper had retired from the Harbor Station, so the Harbor Station had been unstaffed. Coraline Ash lit the lantern of the Harbor Station at sundown for the first time. Pell stood beside Coraline Ash. The wick caught cleanly. The lantern burned mire-essence. The lantern produced mist-light. The mist-light attracted nightmoths. A nightmoth landed on the lantern after sundown. The nightmoth left silken threads on the lantern. Pell joined Coraline Ash at the Harbor Station at midnight and at the third watch.
evtp-000073	On day 590 of Year 2, Coraline Ash told Eli that mire-essence with salt-bloom burns slowly. Coraline Ash told Eli that the cliff-spires shield the Northcove against the north wind. Coraline Ash told Eli that the lanterns of the Northcove burn plain mire-essence. Coraline Ash told Eli that a lantern burning at full rate produces strong mist-light. Eli wrote the answers in her notebook.
