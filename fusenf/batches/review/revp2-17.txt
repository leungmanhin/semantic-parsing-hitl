evtp-000063	On day 455 of Year 2, an apprentice named Eli arrived. Eli came from a Keeper family. The Council assigned Eli to Norren. Norren was a Council member. Norren had not taken an apprentice before. Eli carried Norren's leather case from her second night. Eli did not tend a lantern alone during her first six months. Norren supervised Eli on every night of her first six months.
evtp-000075	On day 620 of Year 2, the night was clear and warm. Keepers counted about twenty-eight nightmoths at each lantern of the Cliff Path. A crowd of nightmoths gathered at the East Row. A crowd of nightmoths gathered at the Middle Row. Sky-cats descended from the cliff-spires. The sky-cats hunted the nightmoth crowds at the East Row and at the Middle Row. Keepers counted about ten nightmoths at the East Row at the third watch. Keepers counted about ten nightmoths at the Middle Row at the third watch. Tessa Brae collected thirty-five feathers the next morning. Hesper distilled four vessels of mire-essence that day.
