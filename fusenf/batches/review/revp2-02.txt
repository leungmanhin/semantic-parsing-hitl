evtp-000052	On day 300 of Year 1, the keepers of the Harbor Row lit the lanterns of the Harbor Row at sundown. The lanterns burned mire-essence. The lanterns produced mist-light. The mist-light attracted nightmoths. A crowd of nightmoths gathered at the Harbor Row. Three sky-cats descended from the cliff-spires. The three sky-cats hunted the nightmoth crowd at the Harbor Row. The nightmoth crowd at the Harbor Row shrank. Each nightmoth that survived the hunt fled to the Hollows. The lanterns at the Hollows gained silken threads that night. The three sky-cats molted during the hunt. Feathers fell to the base of the cliff. Nightmoths left silken threads on the lanterns of the Harbor Row. The keepers of the Harbor Row collected the silken threads from the lanterns at dawn on day 301 of Year 1.
