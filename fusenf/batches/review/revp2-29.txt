evtp-000048	On day 258 of Year 1, a crowd of nightmoths gathered at the Harbor Row. Three sky-cats descended from the cliff-spires. The three sky-cats hunted the nightmoth crowd at the Harbor Row. The nightmoth crowd at the Harbor Row shrank. Coraline Ash counted twenty-four nightmoths at the lantern of the Harbor Station before the hunt. Coraline Ash counted nine nightmoths at the lantern of the Harbor Station after the hunt. Coraline Ash wrote notes about the hunt.
evtp-000066	On day 485 of Year 2, Tam Harrow missed the midnight check at his station on the Middle Row, because his child was ill. Tam Harrow was a junior Keeper. Tam Harrow returned to the station at the third watch. The lantern was burning low. Tam Harrow refilled the mire-essence. Tam Harrow trimmed the wick. The lantern recovered. The station was not at the Sunken Cove. Tam Harrow reported the incident to the Council the next morning. The Council issued no penalty. The Council logged the incident.
